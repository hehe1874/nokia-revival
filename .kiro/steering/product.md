# Product

## Vision

A Nokia feature phone, resurrected in the browser. Not a nostalgia
wallpaper — a working phone: you can type on it, play on it, and make
it ring.

## Who it's for

Anyone who misses the phone that lasted a week on one charge — and
judges who want to see a dead device category come back as something
playable in 30 seconds.

## Core experiences (in priority order)

1. **The phone itself** — the shell has to feel right before anything
   else: screen proportions, keypad layout, D-pad, softkeys.
2. **T9 texting** — type a message with the 2–9 keys. Multi-tap first;
   predictive dictionary if time allows.
3. **Snake** — D-pad / arrow-key play, score, speed-up, game over.
4. **Ringtone composer** — keypad notes, WebAudio playback, save/load.

## Non-goals

- No real calls, SMS, or network features — it's a revival, not a carrier.
- No framework, no build step: `index.html` opens and works.
- No accounts, no backend, no tracking.

## Success criteria

- Opens in a browser with zero setup; the phone is usable in under
  10 seconds.
- Snake is actually fun for a full minute, not a demo loop.
- A first-time visitor can compose and play back a ringtone without
  instructions.
