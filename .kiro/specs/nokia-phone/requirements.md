# Requirements — Nokia phone in the browser

## Phone shell

- R1. Page renders a phone body with screen and 12-key keypad (1–9, *,
  0, #) plus D-pad and two softkeys.
- R2. Screen shows a home screen: signal, battery, clock, menu.
- R3. Arrow keys drive the D-pad; digit keys press the keypad;
  Enter selects. Mouse/touch clicks work too.

## T9 texting

- R4. A Messages app opens from the menu; typing 2–9 enters letters
  by multi-tap (e.g. 2 → a, b, c).
- R5. A sent message appears in the conversation view.

## Snake

- R6. Snake opens from the menu, starts on select, moves with D-pad
  / arrows, eats, grows, speeds up, ends on wall/self collision.
- R7. Score shows on screen; best score persists in localStorage.

## Ringtone composer

- R8. Composer opens from the menu; keypad keys map to notes.
- R9. Notes play immediately via WebAudio; a sequence can be played
  back end-to-end.
- R10. A composed ringtone can be saved and reloaded (localStorage).
